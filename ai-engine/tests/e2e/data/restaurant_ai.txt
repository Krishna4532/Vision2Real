AI SaaS that reduces food waste in restaurants using computer vision, inventory tracking, predictive ordering and waste analytics.

Problem: restaurants lose significant margin from spoilage, over-ordering, and poor inventory visibility.
Target customer: restaurant operators, multi-unit chains, and food service teams.
Solution: a platform that uses computer vision at the back of house, tracks inventory in real time, predicts purchase orders, and surfaces waste analytics to reduce spoilage.
Business model: subscription SaaS for restaurants with implementation and analytics add-ons.

Core features:
- camera-based food inventory counts and shelf-life tracking
- ingredient usage analytics across prep stations and menus
- predictive ordering and purchase recommendation engine
- waste event monitoring and root cause analysis
- integration with POS and ERP systems

Customer value:
- lower food costs
- fewer stockouts
- better purchasing decisions
- reduced labor time spent on manual inventory checks
